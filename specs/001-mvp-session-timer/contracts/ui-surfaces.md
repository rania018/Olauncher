# Contract: user-visible surfaces (MVP)

All text is plain, calm and English. The look reuses upstream theme attributes (light/dark, font,
text size) plus one accent colour (FR-030) for buttons, chips, toggles, the time-left reminder and
notifications. App icons appear only where they identify an app: the duration prompt and the
settings list (owner feedback; see plan.md Complexity Tracking for Constitution IV).

| Surface | Kind | Content | Actions | Must never |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Duration prompt | `PromptActivity` (translucent, own task, excluded from Recents) | App icon, "How long?" + app name; presets as accent chips on one line (wrapping only if they do not fit); custom number field (1–60) with an outlined Open | Preset → launch; custom → launch; **Cancel** (largest) and Back → home. With the accessibility toggle on it also appears over a tracked app opened from the recent-apps list, a link or a notification (FR-031); there a choice keeps the app as it was (no relaunch) and Cancel/Back go home | Appear for exempt or untracked apps; reappear while it is already up for the same app |
| Cooldown screen | `PromptActivity` mode `BLOCKED` | "Instagram is available again at 14:10" | **Go home** (default); "I really need this" → 30 s countdown → 5-min session. Also shown over an app opened from outside the launcher during its cooldown (FR-031) | Hide the override |
| Countdown | Ongoing silent notification (channel `calm_session`, low importance) | "Instagram · 3:42 left" (system chronometer, counting down) | Tap → small session sheet (`PromptActivity` mode `SESSION`) with "End now"; also an "End now" action on the notification | Make sound; be shown when no session runs |
| Warning | Heads-up notification (channel `calm_warning`, high importance, no sound in silent mode) | "1 minute left in Instagram" | Tap → dismiss | Repeat in the same session; show during any call or on the lock screen |
| Time's-up card | Pop-up card over the dimmed app (`TYPE_APPLICATION_OVERLAY`, full-screen translucent scrim, centred card in upstream's dialog style); the app stays visible but receives no touches; media paused with transient audio focus while shown (not during a call) | "Time's up: 5 min in Instagram" | **Go home** (default, filled accent button; Back too); Extend (outlined) → wait 15 s → +2/+5 chips (max 2); "I really need this" → 30 s → +5 min | Show over an exempt or other app, over the lock screen, while the phone rings, or during a call unless the tracked app is confirmed to be in front (FR-018); take audio focus during a call; stay > 120 s untouched |
| Time left on screen | Overlay chosen in settings: **pill** (solid accent label with a shadow and a ring that empties; movable; minutes left, seconds in the last minute; tap → session sheet) or **bar** (6 dp accent line on a dark track under the status bar, shrinking, ignores touches), or off. Both turn amber from the warning on (no pulse or other custom animation). With the accessibility toggle on, shown only while the tracked app is in front; with it off, on every screen while the session runs | "4 min left", "0:42 left" | Drag (pill); tap (pill) | Show when no session runs, while the phone rings, during a call unless the tracked app is confirmed to be in front (FR-018), on the lock screen, together with the time's-up card, or (toggle on) over the home screen or another app |
| Time's-up fallback | High-priority notification (channel `calm_expiry`) | "Time's up in Instagram" | Tap → home | Be used when the overlay permission is granted |
| Health line | One `TextView` on the home screen under the clock/date | e.g. "Timer paused: notifications are off", "Limits apply only from this launcher: app limits are off in Accessibility" | Tap → onboarding page for that item | Show more than one line; use red or alarm wording |
| Onboarding | `OnboardingActivity` (one page per item, including "App limits" for the accessibility toggle, with the restricted-settings tip) | Why / what is reduced / status ✓✗ | Open setting; Skip; Next | Block use of the launcher |
| Screen-time settings | `ScreenTimeSettingsActivity`, opened from one new row in upstream settings, labelled "Screen time limits" (upstream already has a "Screen time" row for its usage display) | Cards: **Permissions and setup** (status, opens onboarding); **Sessions** (session-length chips, tap to remove, "+ Add", at most 6; cooldown, warning and extension wait as −/+ steppers); **Time left on screen** (Pill / Bar / Off toggles with a one-line explanation); **Accent colour** (8 swatches, name of the chosen one); **Apps** (search; rows with icon, name, status line and Track / Exempt toggles that exclude each other; tracked apps first) | Every change saves at once; tap a tracked app → its own lengths and cooldown (Save / Use defaults / Cancel); removing a default exempt app asks for confirmation | Change upstream settings |

Notification channel IDs and the overlay are owned by `app.calmlauncher.enforce` /
`app.calmlauncher.session`. The override countdown resets when the surface loses visibility
(`onPause` for activities, `onWindowVisibilityChanged` for the overlay).
